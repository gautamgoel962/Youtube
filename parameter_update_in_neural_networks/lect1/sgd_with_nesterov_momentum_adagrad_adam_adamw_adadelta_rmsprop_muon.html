Muon orthogonalizes Nesterov momentum, equalizing update scale across directions
SGD: w -= lr × ∇w
Momentum: v = βv - lr×∇w; w += v
Nesterov: v = βv - lr×∇(w+βv); w += v
AdaGrad: G += ∇w²; w -= lr×∇w/√(G+ε)
Adam: m=β₁m+(1-β₁)∇w; v=β₂v+(1-β₂)∇w²; w-=lr·m̂/√(v̂+ε)
AdamW: Adam + decoupled decay; w-=lr·(m̂/√(v̂+ε) + λw)
Adadelta: E[g²]=ρE[g²]+(1-ρ)g²; Δw=-√(E[Δw²]+ε)/√(E[g²]+ε)·g
RMSProp: E[g²]=ρE[g²]+(1-ρ)g²; w-=lr·g/√(E[g²]+ε)
Muon: v=βv+∇(w+βv); w-=lr·v/‖v‖ (orthogonalized Nesterov)
